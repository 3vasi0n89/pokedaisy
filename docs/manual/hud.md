Modern Pokémon games keep the important things on screen while you explore: your team, where you are, what you're carrying. The HUD does the same for the classic games. It's most useful on a device with one screen, where the companion is tucked away in a side panel, but it works with two screens too.

It's on by default. Turn it off, or change how it looks, in `HUD`, in the `SCREEN` group of Settings on either screen.

## What it shows

| Part | What you see | Tap it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party | Each Pokémon's icon, level and HP bar. A status like `PSN` or `SLP` sits on the icon, and a fainted Pokémon is faded with `FNT`. | Open that Pokémon's summary, the same one the `PARTY` tab shows. Up and down step through your team. |
| Minimap | The region map around you, with your head on the spot you're in and the place's name under it. | Open the whole map, with `PLACES` and `FLY`. |
| Poké Balls | The balls in your bag and how many of each. | Open the bag on its `POKé BALLS` pocket. |
| `BAG` | A compact bag: the pockets as tabs and your items as a grid with counts. Pick an item to read about it, and `USE` it where the companion can. | |
| `WILD` | This area's wild Pokémon: by grass, water, fishing rod or rock, with their levels and how often they show up. Ones you haven't seen stay `???`, and a ball marks the ones you've caught. The red number on the button counts the ones you haven't caught yet. | |

The game keeps running while the map, bag, wild list or a summary is open. Tap anywhere around it, or press `BACK`, to close it. With the `GLASS` look these open in see-through panels too.

`WILD` needs the game's own encounter tables, so it shows only for games whose `GUIDE` tab has a `HERE` page. Items the companion can't use (and ones used on a single move) have no `USE` here: use them from the `BAG` tab or in the game.

## When it shows

Only while you're walking around. The first time you play a game, it shows up once you've taken a few steps (PokeDaisy learns what that game's field looks like, and remembers it). It fades out for a battle, a menu screen like the party or the bag, the game's own town map, and a moment later it comes back. With `EXPAND` on it also steps aside for a conversation or a sign. On a phone held upright it's off for now: the companion sits right under the game there.

Without `EXPAND`, the HUD stays on the game's picture, not in the bars beside it (except `RAILS`, which is made for those bars). If the touch pad is showing, the HUD moves its corners in so it never covers the pad's buttons.

On a device with one screen, the button that opens the companion's side panel joins `BAG` and `WILD` as `COMPANION` while the HUD is up, instead of sitting on the screen's edge. When the HUD steps aside (in a battle or a menu), the edge tab is back.

## Make it yours

| Setting | Choices | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HUD` | **`ON`**, `OFF` | The whole HUD. |
| `LAYOUT` | **`CORNERS`**, `CORNERS 2`, `RAILS`, `DOCK` | `CORNERS` puts the minimap top right (the games show a new area's name at the top left), your party bottom left and the rest bottom right. `CORNERS 2` swaps the right side: the minimap bottom right, the balls and buttons top right. `RAILS` lines everything up in two columns at the sides of the screen, in the bars beside the game (or over the extra map with `EXPAND`), so the game's own picture is never covered. `DOCK` puts your party, balls and buttons in one bar along the bottom, and the minimap top right. |
| `LOOK` | **`GLASS`**, `WINDOW` | `GLASS` is a dark see-through panel that lets the game show through. `WINDOW` uses the white windows the rest of the app has. |
| `SIZE` | `SMALL`, **`NORMAL`**, `LARGE` | How big it is. Each step changes its pixels by one whole screen pixel, so the pixel art stays sharp. |
| `PARTY`, `MINIMAP`, `POKé BALLS`, `BAG`, `WILD POKéMON` | **`ON`**, `OFF` | Show or hide each part. |
